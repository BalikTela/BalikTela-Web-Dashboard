1. Rules That Apply Across Pages

  • Mass: stored/calculated in kilograms; displayed as metric tons: t = kg / 1,000. Most mass labels use up to three decimal places; values under 1 kg may use six decimals in tons. CSV details retain up to six decimal places in tons. Percentages generally use one decimal place; predictive evaluation commonly uses two. Calculate from unrounded weights, not displayed labels.
  • Material cohort: most analytics select non-archived batches by their registration timestamp, then use linked evidence known through the current as-of time. August chart values mean August-registered batches, not necessarily collections physically performed in August. Selecting a historical period does not reconstruct the historical status of every batch.
  • Timezone: reporting boundaries are Asia/Manila. Default Last 3 calendar months means current month to date plus the preceding two full calendar months. In October 2026, the window starts 1 August 2026.
  • Comparison: normally the immediately preceding window of equal elapsed duration. Year to date compares with the same date/time in the previous year, with leap-day adjustment. All time/Pilot period has no preceding comparison.
  • Access and filters: authorized account, organization, Hub and regional scope apply before aggregation. Material, pathway, transaction and evidence filters can further change the cohort. A card is not automatically a platform-wide total.
  • Missing values: Pending evidence, Not available, Intake not recorded and Feed unavailable are not measured zeros. Zero may be displayed when a supported input/ledger exists and no qualifying quantity is present. Missing declared weights are omitted from the declared sum and counted separately.
  • PoC scope: synthetic scenario lifecycle records and shared operational records are combined under the same material-evidence rules. They do not become independently measured real-world or national totals. The predictive training dataset is separate.
  • Overlapping stages: declared, transferred, collected, sorted, reused and recycled weights are different views of the same material. Do not add all stages to calculate a larger total.
  • Pagination: totals normally use the complete filtered record set, not only the ten rows currently shown. Search/filter behavior is noted below where summary cards use a different set from the table.

Sources: shared/metrics.ts, shared/reporting.ts, server/dashboardAnalytics.ts.

3. Overview: Main Tiles

FigureImplemented calculation and interpretation
Total Textiles Declaredsum(declaredWeightKg once per selected registered batch) / 1,000. Total material weight entered into the platform, including estimated or measured declarations. The evidence summary and Material Flow report use this same label and calculation under identical filters. No receipt or completed transfer is required; missing weights are excluded. The headline sparkline uses monthly declarations, not physical intake. This replaces the former Total Textiles Collected headline.
Batches with validated intakeNumber of unique selected batches with an accepted collection weight. A missing receipt does not become a zero-weight collection.
Recyclable Textiles Receivedsum(accepted intake of recyclable-eligible batches) / 1,000. Eligible means recorded condition Recyclable, or a Consumer source with a posted non-Draft/non-Unknown own listing. The second is a workflow assumption, not technical certification. Only count it after intake qualifies; a declaration alone is not physical receipt.
Recyclable / assumed batch countsCount collected batches passing that classification; the assumed subset counts those eligible via Consumer listing rather than an explicit Recyclable condition.
Completed Textile TransfersFor each unique Completed/Processed transfer, prefer its one current Verified Validation measurement with verifier; otherwise use its reported receipt weight. Sum known final weights and divide by 1,000. Never substitute agreed weight for a completed transfer. Repeated movements of the same textile can occur.
Weighted / missing final weightCount completed transfers with a qualifying validation or receipt weight; missing = all completed transfers minus those with weight. Reported-receipt count identifies transfers using the fallback.
Recorded Hub Disposalsum(valid distinct disposal-ledger weights) / 1,000. Includes archived material in the selected Hub registration cohort. Partial disposals count once per ledger ID. Do not add listing cumulative disposal counters. Missing feed and invalid-only evidence are not published as a supported zero.
Confirmed Recovery Share100 * matched final reuse-and-recycling output / validated collection, rounded to one decimal. Numerator includes only outcomes linked to collected batches. Pending/unconfirmed collected material remains in the denominator; unlinked final outputs are excluded. Missing denominator, no matching outcome evidence or unresolved allocation conflicts makes the percentage unavailable.
Registered AccountsDistinct valid Supabase-auth UUIDs attached to scoped profiles, joined by the as-of time; exclude SYN-prefixed profile IDs. Includes real test registrations and is not a count of recently active or logged-in users. Geography, role and organization filters apply; material category and cohort registration dates do not determine this account count.
Active ProducersWithin each represented batch region, count distinct source user IDs currently assigned Producer role; add regional counts. This means participating source accounts, not sessions or all registered Producers. The same account in multiple regions can be counted more than once across the total.
Active RecyclersWithin each represented batch region, count distinct recipient IDs currently assigned Recycler role, excluding accounts recognized as Hub operators; add regional counts. Transfers do not have to be completed. Cross-region involvement can repeat one account. This is not the merged Consumer/Recycler directory count.
Completed TransactionsCount distinct selected transfers whose status is Completed or Processed, regardless of whether final weight is available.
Change versus previous period100 * (current count - previous count) / previous count, one decimal. If prior count is zero/non-positive, show No baseline activity or No change, not an invented percentage increase.
Example from earlier screenshots, not a fresh live read:
105,727 kg / 1,000 = 105.727 t collected.
(19,020 kg reuse + 48,319 kg recycling) / 1,000 = 67.339 t total confirmed output.
Of that, 86 kg has no matching intake. Therefore (67,339 - 86) / 105,727 * 100 = 63.6%, not 67.339 / 105.727.
105,727 - 67,253 = 38.474 t collected with recovery unconfirmed.

4. Material Evidence Summary and Acceptance Rules

FigureComputation
Total Textiles Declaredsum(declaredWeightKg for unique selected batches). Missing values omitted, not evidence of physically received zero. Original material reported to the platform, not national textile generation. Same total as the Overview headline.
Textiles Physically ReceivedAccepted physical intake once per batch from validated Collection output, otherwise one qualifying current Verified receipt with a verifier and matching transfer. Separate from Total Textiles Declared; retained for lifecycle recovery calculations. Internal collectedKg field names remain compatible with the shared integration.
Sorted outputSum the output weight of one accepted validated Sorting record per batch. Input and output must be valid non-negative masses and output cannot exceed input by more than 0.001 kg. Multiple Sorting records make that batch's sorted amount unsupported.
Confirmed reuseSum accepted final output for Reuse, Repair, Redistribution and Upcycling outcomes. These are grouped under reuse in the current implementation.
Confirmed recyclingSum accepted final output explicitly marked Recycling. This is different from recyclable intake.
Received: recovery unconfirmedFor each accepted intake batch: intake - matching accepted final output; sum the batch remainders. Withhold conflicting outcomes and retain their intake as unresolved. May include pending stock, rejects, process losses and missing records; not measured disposal or warehouse inventory.
Missing intakeselected unique batches - batches with accepted intake.
Unlinked outcomeAccepted final output for batches without accepted intake. Included in total final output, excluded from recovery-share numerator.
Collection/outcome conflictsCounts of detected ambiguous allocations: repeated collection evidence, unsupported remainder accounting, duplicate final portions, conflicting final records, or outcome input above accepted intake. Not a sum of disputed kilograms.

When Final Output Is Accepted

The Processing/Recovery record must be Validated with a validator; reference a Completed/Processed transfer for the same batch; have valid input/output/reject/loss quantities; and balance within 0.001 kg. Its input cannot exceed a known verified transfer allocation. A final Recovery record supersedes a Processing record for the same transfer/outcome rather than being added again. Multiple separate transfer/outcome portions on one batch are conservatively withheld until a valid root-lot allocation contract exists.

Collection acceptance is not identical to final-outcome mass balancing: it checks an unambiguous Collection input/output pair or a qualifying verified receipt. Hub handling also accepts certain Measured receipts, so its supported scope is not identical to platform-wide validated collection.

Source: server/materialEvidence.ts, shared/recovery.ts, server/hubEvidence.ts.

Hub Management / Directory

The submission/disposal cards appear only in Hub Collection & Disposal. Hub Disposals provides the ledger without repeating them. Handling includes archived history; match the same batch population when comparing with Overview's active declared total.

Its table search does not recompute summary cards.

The Processing Records tab and its stage-record forms have been removed from Recovery Monitoring. This page contains only Hub collection, management/directory and disposal. Existing stage evidence remains stored and continues supporting analytics, data quality and reports under the acceptance rules in Section 4; hiding the interface does not delete or reclassify that evidence.

Example: Hub receipt 100 kg, disposal 25 kg, supported transfer out 15 kg. Awaiting Disposal = 100 - 25 - 15 = 60 kg (0.06 t). Without the receipt or supported outflow, the system cannot safely publish that balance.

FigureComputation
Registered HubsCount matching non-Inactive Hub registry records. Aggregate-only users see regional Hub group count, not the number of individual Hubs.
Storage capacityConfigured positive kilograms from facilities.storage_capacity_kg, entered in tons in the Hub registry. Independent of monthly handling capacity and reporting dates. Existing demo Hubs without a configured value retain labeled illustrative limits from shared/hubStorage.ts. Operational Hubs without a value are unavailable, not assumed to have 1,000 kg capacity.

Incoming reservationsOriginal declared kilograms of published, pending consumer batches routed to the Hub, counted once per batch. Physical receipt replaces the reservation rather than adding it again. Unknown weights withhold availability.

AvailabilityAvailable only with an approved assigned operator, operational Hub and complete balances below the 90% allocated threshold. At or above that threshold: Intake paused. Maintenance, inactive and onboarding conditions override reopening. Full disposal reaches 0% only if no stock remains; incoming reservations may still keep intake paused.
Network utilization100 * sum(stock held) / sum(storage capacity), not the mean of percentages. Any incomplete balance withholds the network utilization.
Recorded disposalAll-history unique disposal ledger weights attributed to this physical Hub, not recycling output. This may differ from Recovery Monitoring's selected registration cohort.
Overview chart / capacity insightUses the same hubStorage rows as Hub Facilities. Headroom insights consider selectable Hubs only. Regional aggregates are never individually selectable.
Labeled Hub simulationThe small SYN-HUBSIM-V1- dataset links seven existing Hubs to synthetic consumers, intake and disposal. Disabled non-login simulation operators may show modeled capacity states, labeled Simulation only. They gain no permission, approval, Auth account or mobile destination eligibility; selectable remains false. Headroom includes explicitly labeled modeled space, not a promise of bookable mobile capacity.
Example: 1,000 kg illustrative storage, 900 kg stock, no reservations = 90%, Intake paused. Record a supported 400 kg disposal: 500 kg stock, 50%, Available, 400 kg intake headroom. Dispose the remaining 500 kg: 0%, Available. History is retained, not reset.

The legacy period-handling payload remains historical throughput, not storage availability. No mobile selection or atomic admission enforcement is implemented by this dashboard-only calculation. 12. Advanced Analytics

Diagnostic

Intake evidence pending = selected batches without accepted intake. Transactions completed = completed / all scoped transfers * 100. Highest utilization = maximum displayed Hub utilization where the selected top row has capacity. Lowest confirmed share = smallest available category recovery share. Collected stock without confirmed recovery and Confirmed recovery share reuse the central evidence figures; they are not independent datasets.

Predictive: Data Basis

9,600 synthetic event records = 120 weeks * 4 regions * 20 records per region-week. They aggregate to 120 weekly time points, not 9,600 independent weeks. Training = 84 weeks / 6,720 records (70%); validation = 18 / 1,440 (15%); test = 18 / 1,440 (15%). Snapshot cutoff is 12 October 2026 exclusive, so history is through 11 October. Region/material/access filters select rows; history view 13/26/52/120 changes displayed history only, not the training window.

Scoped forecasts require at least 600 records and 104 active weeks (positive declared weekly mass). This threshold is a PoC gate, not real-world validation. New operational CSV imports do not retrain this fixed model.

Predictive figureImplemented calculation
Weekly historical declared / collectedSum the corresponding synthetic event mass within each week after scope filters; round to 0.01 kg. Declared waste means declared platform material, not total Philippine waste.
Candidate forecast methodsLast-value: repeat latest weekly value. Trailing-mean: mean of last 8 weeks. Linear-trend: ordinary least squares on last 26 weeks, extrapolated 1-4 weeks. Seasonal-naive: corresponding week 52 weeks earlier. All predictions floored at zero.
Selected modelChoose the method with lowest rolling-origin validation MAE, separately for declared and collected targets. Every origin sees earlier observations only. Final four-week forecasts refit the chosen method on all 120 observed weeks.
Projected collection / declared wasteFour weekly point forecasts summed before display rounding. Collection point forecast per week is constrained not to exceed that week's declared forecast. This does not prove future recycling or disposal.
Weekly range lower / upperFor each horizon, use the 80th percentile of absolute validation forecast errors as a symmetric error width: lower = max(0, point - width), upper = point + width. Collection upper is additionally bounded by the declared upper. These are empirical nominal-80% weekly ranges, not a guaranteed confidence level or interval for the four-week sum.
Test MAEsum(abs(predicted - actual)) / prediction count. Shown in tons. Test uses 15 rolling origins * 4 horizons = 60 overlapping predictions for each target; not 60 independent weeks.
Test WAPE100 * sum(abs(predicted - actual)) / sum(actual). Zero actual total = unavailable. Lower indicates smaller mass-weighted error.
Test biasmean(predicted - actual) in kg, stored in evaluation even when not a headline. Positive overpredicts; negative underpredicts.
Test range coverage100 * test predictions whose actual lies in the modeled weekly range / all test predictions. Report observed coverage; do not assume it equals 80%.
Material selection likelihood(selected offers + 1) / (offered records + 2) for each category, displayed as a percentage. This is Beta(1,1) smoothing. Categories are independent conditional likelihoods and do not sum to 100%.
Selected / offeredRaw counts for each category in the recent model-selected window. Choose window from 8, 13, 26 or 52 weeks by lowest validation log loss.
Highest selection likelihoodCategory with maximum smoothed probability in that recent window. It does not identify any particular real recycler's preference.
Observed selected share100 * category selected / all category selections in recent window. Unlike selection likelihood, this is a composition share.
Change versus prior window100 * (recent selected / recent offered - previous selected / previous offered), in percentage points. Uses raw rates, not smoothed probabilities; unavailable if either category window lacks offers.
12-week trendObserved selected / offered for that category in each of the last 12 weeks. Missing weekly offers = gap, not a zero preference.
Test Brier scoremean((predicted probability - observed 0-or-1 selection)^2). Lower is better, zero perfect. Pooled baseline Brier uses the category-blind smoothed historical selection rate for comparison.
Test log loss / held-out offers-mean(y * ln(p) + (1-y) * ln(1-p)); count evaluated event records in final 18 test weeks. Lower loss is better. Does not imply validated performance on real data.

Sources: AdvancedAnalytics.tsx, predictive.ts, synthetic.ts, PredictiveAnalytics.tsx.

18. Important Interpretation and Consistency Caveats

These are audit observations, not fixes made by this document. The core lifecycle totals share an aggregator, but not every displayed number has the same population, evidence standard or time basis.
  1. Active Producers/Recyclers are regional involvements. Summing regional unique IDs can count the same account more than once across regions. Current Recycler KPI uses exact Recycler role, while directory presentation merges Consumers and Recyclers. It is not a globally unique merged-user KPI.
  2. Registered-account definitions differ. Overview deduplicates valid auth-linked profiles; User Accounts currently uses returned profile count and an exact legacy verification label. Do not use them interchangeably as security approval totals.
  3. There is a tolerance inconsistency. Data Quality's stage-balance warning uses 0.01 kg; final outcome acceptance and record-detail balance use 0.001 kg. A 0.005 kg mismatch can be rejected by the latter without the former raising its imbalance finding.
  4. There are multiple denominators. Declared outcome gap subtracts outputs from declarations; collected recovery-unconfirmed subtracts matched outputs from intake; awaiting Hub disposal subtracts disposal and evidenced outflow from physical Hub receipts. These answer different questions.
  5. Monthly throughput and storage are separate. Hub Facilities uses configured physical storage limits and all-history stock, with labeled illustrative fallback only for existing demo Hubs. Legacy prorated monthly handling metrics remain historical workload only, never admission availability. Missing stock evidence is not an empty Hub.
  6. Confirmed total output can exceed the matched numerator. An accepted final record without matching intake contributes to output totals, not recovery share. Sorting yield further restricts its denominator to batches with matching sorted/final evidence.
  7. Current stock is not cumulative intake. Material Registry Current can be zero after transfer/closure while earlier collection remains valid. Historical measurement sums would double-count material.
  8. Account, audit and material dates differ. Accounts are an as-of count; material activity follows batch cohorts; exports follow audit event dates. Hub Directory uses recorded history. Filter differences can be intentional rather than arithmetic errors.
  9. Charts cannot establish causation. A rising registration-cohort line, a category correlation or a synthetic forecast does not prove real environmental savings. Removed environmental metrics are unavailable, not zero.
  10. Not every input is independently measured. Profile volume and trust are reported/placeholder fields. Synthetic records remain synthetic. Combined PoC totals and synthetic evaluation scores should not be described as independently measured national or real-world results.

Recommended reconciliation practice: compare the same actor scope, batch cohort, filters and as-of time; trace a number to its batch/transfer/measurement IDs; use raw kilograms; recompute the ratio from its own numerator and denominator; then apply display rounding.

This reference reflects the declared-headline and physical-receipt label updates. Receipt-based recovery calculations, mobile code and shared database records were not changed. Historical screenshot numbers are not a statement of current database totals.
